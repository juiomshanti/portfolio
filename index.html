About

Senior Product Designer with end-to-end ownership across 0→1 AI and digital products.

I turn ambiguous customer and business problems into clear product strategy, trusted workflows, scalable systems, and shipped experiences. I am currently on a mission with The CoBuilders to help small blue-collar business owners reach their first 1 million.

I have 5+ years of experience working across product discovery, strategy, research, UX, UI, prototyping, and delivery—partnering closely with founders and engineers to define the right problem, make fast trade-offs, and ship high-quality experiences. Having a developer background, I know how to use code and AI-assisted prototyping tools to move quickly, test ideas, and collaborate more effectively with engineering.

I’m particularly interested in working with teams building ambitious AI-native products and services where thoughtful product design can make complexity feel clear, capable, and human. I’m most at home in fast-moving environments—highly collaborative teams, incomplete information, high ownership, and decisions that need to be made quickly.
